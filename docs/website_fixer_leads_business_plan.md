# Website Fixer Lead Generation — Business Plan (MVP)

**Goal:** Generate qualified local leads by sending **short, evidence-based “quick wins” audits** to Cleveland-area businesses, then upsell to paid quick-fix work and longer engagements.

**Operating constraints (by design):**
- Low volume at first (**3–5 sends/day**) to avoid deliverability problems and bad reputation.
- Automated research/audits; **human approves every send** (draft + send + follow-ups).
- Email outreach only (MVP).
- Always honor opt-outs and maintain a suppression list.

---

## 1) Positioning

### Value proposition
> “I looked at your site and found a few fast fixes that can improve speed, trust, and search visibility — I’ll send the exact steps for free, or I can do the fixes for you.”

Why this works:
- You lead with **useful proof** and reduce the perceived “sales pitch”.
- You sell **time saved** (you already did the diagnosis).
- You start with **quick fixes** (low friction) before pitching deeper work.

### Why Cleveland + local-first
Local businesses respond better to:
- Local reference points (“Cleveland”, “nearby”, “in the area”).
- Practical improvements tied to calls, bookings, and local search.

---

## 2) Ideal customer profile (pick 2 to start)

You want businesses where:
- A small site improvement quickly turns into calls/leads (high ROI).
- Website quality strongly affects trust and conversion.

Good starting categories:
- Home services: HVAC, plumbing, roofing, electricians
- Medical: dentists, orthodontists, med spas
- Legal: small law firms
- Auto: repair shops / collision
- Specialty services: gyms, tutoring, remodeling

**MVP decision needed:** choose **two** categories for the first 2 weeks.

---

## 3) Offer ladder (sell “small” first)

### Step 1 — Free micro-audit (email)
- 2–3 bullet findings, each backed by proof.
- One simple CTA: “Want the step-by-step fixes?”

### Step 2 — Quick Fix Pack (paid)
A clear, bounded service:
- Fix HTTPS redirect issues / mixed content
- Remove or repair top broken links
- Contact page improvements (phone/email visibility, click-to-call)
- Basic SEO hygiene (titles/meta descriptions for key pages)
- Fast performance wins (image compression, caching hints, script cleanup)

Target: 1–3 hours, flat fee.

### Step 3 — Monthly maintenance (optional)
- Monitoring, small fixes, updates, backups, basic SEO upkeep

### Step 4 — Bigger work (only after trust)
- Rebuild, deeper SEO, conversion optimization

---

## 4) Outreach strategy (MVP)

### Volume + pace
- Start at **3–5 sends/day** for 2–3 weeks.
- Increase only after:
  - Low bounce rate
  - Some replies
  - No spam complaints
  - Warm sender reputation

### Email tone rules
- Short, human, direct.
- No marketing fluff.
- No fake personalization.
- **No claims without proof**.

### Compliance (non-negotiable)
For US commercial email, you must:
- Use accurate headers/sender identity
- Avoid deceptive subject lines
- Provide a **clear opt-out** and honor it quickly
- Include a **valid physical postal address** (street address or PO box)

If you don’t have a business address you want to share, get a PO box.

---

## 5) Lead sources (MVP-safe)

### Recommended sources
- Official APIs (e.g., Google Places API) to find businesses and their websites
- Public directories with explicit permission/terms that allow use
- Manual additions (referrals, local lists)

### Avoid
- Scraping Google Maps listings directly (high takedown risk)
- Any “grey area” scraping that violates terms

---

## 6) Metrics that matter

Track these weekly:
- Leads audited
- Emails drafted
- Emails sent
- Delivery/bounces (if available)
- Replies
- Qualified conversations
- Paid quick-fix conversions
- Average revenue per lead

Early success signal: *replies* (even “not interested”) with low bounces.

---

## 7) Risk controls

### Deliverability & reputation
- Throttle sends (already planned)
- Suppression list for opt-outs and bounces
- Don’t email role accounts that are often spam traps if you can avoid it (e.g., “admin@”)

### Accuracy & trust
- Store evidence (screenshots, exact broken URLs, lighthouse summary)
- If an audit is uncertain, don’t include the claim.

---

## 8) 14-day MVP plan

### Days 1–2: Setup
- Notion board (pipeline stages + properties)
- Basic lead discovery for Cleveland in 2 categories
- Suppression list storage

### Days 3–7: Audits + draft workflow
- Automated audit pipeline
- LLM summarization + email drafting
- Human review workflow in Notion

### Days 8–14: Controlled sending
- 3–5/day sends
- Monitor bounces/replies
- Iterate email format and audit checks

---

## 9) What you still need to decide

1) Two starting categories in Cleveland  
2) Your sender identity (domain/email)  
3) Physical address to include (PO box is fine)  
4) Follow-up policy (MVP suggestion: **no automated follow-ups** for the first 2 weeks)
